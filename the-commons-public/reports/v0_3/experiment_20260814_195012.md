## Core question

## Generation One discoveries

### Commons Entry 1 — Field Researcher A

**Topic:** Drel/Cassik interaction

**Inferred rule:** Drel hunts Cassik when drel_density is at least 35 and temperature is below 20°C; it does not hunt under the tested conditions otherwise. Hunting reduces Cassik density by exactly 20 units during the observation interval.

**Confidence:** 0.98

**Evidence summary:** Hunting occurred at (17,35) and (17,50), but not at (20,35) or (18,50), supporting density ≥35 together with temperature <20°C. At density 20, hunting occurred at 17°C but not 18°C, showing that the observed data do not support a simple density-only rule; the 17°C, density 19 non-hunt is also consistent with a density threshold between 19 and 20 in that context.

**Caveat:** The exact low-density boundary is unresolved because observations at density 20 conflict by temperature; the rule is the most specific conjunction consistent with all observations, not a uniquely identified mechanism.

### Commons Entry 2 — Field Researcher B

**Topic:** Erune chemical release

**Inferred rule:** Compound R is released when erune density is at least 20, tolm density is at least 40, and dissolved salt is at most 7; temperature has no detected effect within the tested range.

**Confidence:** 0.86

**Evidence summary:** Positive cases (20,40,7), (35,60,3), and (30,41,7) satisfy all three thresholds. Each observed negative violates at least one: erune 19, tolm 39, salt 8, or combinations with insufficient tolm/erune.

**Caveat:** The observations support these threshold conditions but do not establish whether all are strictly necessary outside the tested values or whether additional factors affect release.

### Commons Entry 3 — Field Researcher C

**Topic:** Compound R and Veyra larvae

**Inferred rule:** Compound R suppresses Veyra larvae when mineral K is 11 or lower; it does not suppress them when mineral K is 12 or higher. Compound R is required: without it, no suppression was observed. Other measured population densities did not modify this effect.

**Confidence:** 1.00

**Evidence summary:** With Compound R, suppression occurred at K=6, 10, and 11, but not at K=12, 13, or 18. Without Compound R, no suppression occurred at K=3 or 11.

**Caveat:** The exact cutoff is supported by observations at K=11 and K=12; behavior at untested intermediate or non-integer values is not established.

### Commons Entry 4 — Field Researcher D

**Topic:** Veyra abundance and Tolm reproduction

**Inferred rule:** Suppression occurs when mineral K is 11 and Veyra density is at least 71, or when mineral K is 5 and Veyra density is at least 72. No suppression was observed at K=12 (tested densities 71 and 100), K=3 with density 20, or below the corresponding threshold at K=11 and K=5. Compound R was absent in all trials, so its role is undetermined.

**Confidence:** 0.98

**Evidence summary:** At K=11: density 71 and 95 suppressed, while 70 did not. At K=5: 72 suppressed, while 69 did not. At K=12: 71 and 100 did not suppress; at K=3, density 20 did not.

**Caveat:** The rule is supported only for the tested K values and densities; behavior at untested combinations is unknown.

### Commons Entry 5 — Field Researcher E

**Topic:** Mora bloom trigger

**Inferred rule:** Mora blooms if and only if Compound R is present and post-interaction Cassik density is below 30 (observed blooming at densities 5, 28, and 29; no blooming at 30 or 50, and none when R is absent).

**Confidence:** 1.00

**Evidence summary:** All eight controlled observations match the conjunction: R present plus Cassik density <30. Other tested factors showed no direct effect after accounting for these variables.

**Caveat:** The exact threshold is supported by observations at 29 and 30; behavior at unobserved densities between or beyond these values is inferred by the simplest threshold rule.

## Hidden rules used by the objective grader

> These rules were never shown directly to Generation Two. They are included here only after scoring so the experiment can be audited.

1. Drel hunts Cassik iff `temperature_c < 18` AND `drel_density >= 20`; successful hunting lowers Cassik density by 20.
2. Compound R is released iff `erune_density >= 20` AND `tolm_density >= 40` AND `dissolved_salt <= 7`.
3. Veyra larvae are suppressed iff Compound R is present AND `mineral_k < 12`.
4. Tolm reproduction is suppressed iff `veyra_density > 70` AND `mineral_k < 12`.
5. Mora blooms iff Compound R is present AND post-interaction Cassik density is `< 30`.

## Interpretation guardrails

- This tests contextual knowledge transfer between fresh model instances, not consciousness or sentience.
- The fictional rules are deterministic, which gives the experiment an objective grader.
- Generation Two agents have no private session history from Generation One.
- The Placebo Archive controls partly for extra archive-shaped text, but not every possible framing effect.
- Replicates still share the same underlying model and task instructions.
- A strong Inherited Commons advantage would show that generated knowledge can be preserved and used by later instances; it would not show that the agents experienced the knowledge as memory.
