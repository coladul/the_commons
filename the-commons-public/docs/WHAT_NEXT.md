# What should happen next?

Do not immediately add a more elaborate v0.6 just because v0.5 was interesting.

Recommended sequence:

1. **Freeze v0.1–v0.5.** Preserve the reports and scripts as historical artifacts.
2. **Get adversarial review.** Ask multiple independent humans and models to look for confounds.
3. **Open issues for criticisms.** Turn each serious criticism into a testable hypothesis.
4. **Choose v0.6 from the strongest criticism.** The next experiment should be designed to potentially destroy the current interpretation.
5. **Preregister the confirmatory design.** Before running a large v0.6/v0.7, write down the generator, sample size, contrasts, exclusion rules, and analysis.
6. **Broaden models and worlds.** Test different model families and richer hidden structures only after the simpler design is clean.

Candidate confirmatory directions after review:

- token-length-matched provenance controls;
- shuffled or misleading provenance;
- multiple disagreeing ancestors;
- noisy evidence and probabilistic worlds;
- richer causal graphs rather than threshold rules;
- cross-model inheritance (one model writes, another inherits);
- an automated verifier that checks whether a proposed rule actually fits the cited evidence before it enters The Commons.
